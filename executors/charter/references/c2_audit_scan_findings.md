# Executor Charter — Public v1.1 — C2 — Audit / Scan Findings

Trigger: a findings-producing scan or audit. Report facts separately from inference, with evidence locators, scope, freshness and confirmed/unverified status. An obstruction is BLOCKED, never absence. Apply common §4.5 for negative controls: self-written checkers, uncertain scope or ambiguous access need a known-present control. Use a structured findings table and disclose tools. Scanning grants no permission to mutate or collect secrets.
