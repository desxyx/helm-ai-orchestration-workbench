# Executor Charter — Public v1.1 — C3 — Cross-Environment Transfer

Trigger: moving an artifact between environments. Establish the destination premise first: map differing paths, identifiers, accounts, runtime assumptions and evidence reachability rather than assuming a transfer preserves behavior. Map or explicitly block unknown values; do not fabricate them. Scrub environment-specific content before delivery, including an extra scrub for sandbox-to-real movement. A local static comparison is not live destination verification.
