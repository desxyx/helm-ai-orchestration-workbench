# Human reading guide

Read the case README and timeline first. Choose a phase, then open the actual Council, Executor or UserOps body linked by its record ID. The phase pages and layer indexes cross-link canonical bodies; they do not replace them. The JSON record index includes ordered parts for large native transcripts.

To examine a claim, follow its source ID and relevant message/event pointer in the alignment index. Keep the surrounding prompt, reply order and capture status. Look for later corrections rather than editing an earlier claim in your head. A copied or manually relayed message does not establish an independent participant or automatic delivery.

For W2, start with the revised comparison and then inspect each original run's acceptance, Observer and custodian records. Read raw FAIL with effective UNVERIFIED, null with missing evidence, and every efficiency figure with same-run acceptance and false-success limits. For W3, follow the Windows intake, native use, subjective feedback and later product patches separately.

The omissions manifest explains why some source IDs have no public body. W1's available retained exports are partial custody evidence; absence of the full native original does not authorize reconstructing it. Image descriptions preserve visible facts with limits and do not function as new screenshots or rerun checks.
