The Human Operator / Chair makes task decisions and manually forwards messages. Council Member A/B/C are stable decision-seat aliases; captured provider and model declarations remain in individual records and are not inferred from the aliases. Operations Coordinator sessions maintain state/routing, with Mac and Windows contexts distinguished. Executor, Reviewer, Deployer, Observer, Rapid Context Auditor and implementation-worker actors have distinct scoped roles even where an actor number recurs.
